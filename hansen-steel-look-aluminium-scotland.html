The appearance of steel

Engineered in aluminium

Hansen Products manufactures ultra-slim aluminium window and door systems created to achieve the refined appearance associated with traditional steel glazing. The frames are aluminium—not steel—providing the visual character of narrow steel-style profiles with the practical advantages of a modern aluminium system.

NÜ-LINE Architectural Glazing supports projects across Scotland with product selection, configuration advice, glazing coordination, supply planning and specialist installation. We can become involved early, helping architects, contractors, developers and homeowners assess the right arrangement before details are fixed.

Applications

Steel-look aluminium windows and doors for distinctive architecture

Heritage and conservation-led design

Slim profiles and flexible glazing-bar layouts can complement the proportions of older industrial, civic and residential buildings while introducing modern aluminium construction.

Contemporary residential projects

Fixed screens, opening windows and slimline doors create strong architectural rhythm, generous daylight and a crisp industrial-style finish.

Hospitality and commercial spaces

Suitable configurations can help define restaurants, hotels, reception spaces, offices and mixed-use developments where glazing is central to the design intent.

Arches and bespoke forms

Curved heads, shaped frames and decorative glazing bars provide additional design freedom for individual façades and carefully detailed openings.
